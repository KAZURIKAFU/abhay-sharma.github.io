I'm a final-year B.Tech (Data Science) student at Manipal University Jaipur, working at the intersection of IoT, Big Data, and machine learning. My work spans smart-grid and utility monitoring systems, large-scale sensor data pipelines, edge analytics, and energy-harvesting hardware — backed by 2 patents filed & published with the Indian Patent Office and 3 international research publications.

I've led national-level events as Student Organiser for SIH 2023 & 2024 Grand Finales and served as Convener for ICIDS 2025 and Co-Convener for ICIDS 2024 — conferences that shaped my understanding of both technical depth and collaborative execution.

I'm currently open to Data / IoT / ML Engineer roles in Dubai, Singapore, and India. I bring hands-on experience with GCP, Big Data tooling, and full-stack ML deployment — and the ambition to keep building things that scale.
